# The Floating Overlay

The **Floating Overlay** is Macrion's command center. It floats quietly on top of whatever app or game is currently open, giving you full control over your automations without having to switch apps back and forth.

---

## Anatomy of the Overlay

The overlay appears as a compact vertical toolbar on your screen edge. It provides quick access to common actions:

| Button | Action | What it does |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| ▶ | **Play** | Starts executing the loaded scenario. |
| ⏸ | **Pause** | Temporarily halts actions at the current step. Press Play to resume. |
| ⏹ | **Stop** | Stops execution and resets any active loop counters or scenario states. |
| 🎯 | **Add / Move Targets** | Displays numbered touch target markers on screen that you can drag into position. |
| 🔀 | **Scenario Switcher** | Switch quickly between different saved scenarios without returning to the main app. |
| ⚙️ | **Settings** | Opens quick configuration options for the active scenario. |
| ✖ | **Close** | Exits the overlay and returns to the home screen. |

---

## Moving and Docking

- **Dragging**: Touch and hold the drag handle (top or edge of the bar) to slide the overlay anywhere along the left or right edges of your screen.
- **Collapsing / Minimizing**: Tap the collapse arrow to shrink the toolbar into a tiny bubble icon. This prevents the bar from obscuring health bars, menus, or gameplay controls. Tap the bubble to expand it again.

::: tip Landscape & Portrait Support
Macrion automatically adapts when your phone rotates between portrait and landscape modes, keeping the overlay anchored neatly to the screen edge.
:::

---

## Interactive Target Markers

When configuring click or swipe locations directly on top of your app:

1. Tap the **Targets** button on the overlay.
2. Numbered circles (e.g., `①`, `②`) will appear over your screen.
3. Drag each circle by its center to place it exactly on top of the desired button.
4. For swipe gestures, two connected markers (`① Start` and `① End`) will be shown. Drag both to set the path of the swipe.
5. Tap the checkmark to save the new coordinates.

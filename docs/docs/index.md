# Macrion Documentation

Welcome to the Macrion documentation! Here you will find clear guides explaining how to install, configure, and make the most out of Macrion on your Android device.

---

## What is Macrion?

**Macrion** is an open-source Android automation app designed to automate screen interactions. It can work in two ways:

1. **Simple (Position-Based) Clicking**: Standard auto-clicking where you place tap points on the screen and set intervals (e.g., tap here every 2 seconds).
2. **Smart (Image-Aware) Automation**: Advanced automation that captures the screen, looks for specific icons, buttons, or visual states, and performs actions only when matching conditions are met.
